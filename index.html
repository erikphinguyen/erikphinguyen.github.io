01 — ABOUT

One engineering mindset, three disciplines.

My path started in a chemical engineering lab, running water-filtration experiments at Stanford University, and has taken me through aerospace and medical-device quality systems into full-stack software development. Each stop added a different lens for solving problems: process rigor from chemical engineering, root-cause discipline from quality engineering, and systems thinking from writing code.

At Alloy Processing, I revived a five-year-dormant certification project and closed it within a single quarter, securing $500K/year in new business. At Hydrafacial, I rebuilt the electronic quality management system and cut unnecessary user accounts by 80%, saving the company $480K a year. Today, at Boeing EnCore Interiors, I manage roughly 100 concurrent supplier change notices across 50+ active and legacy aircraft interior programs — keeping commercial interiors airworthy and in production.

Outside of engineering, I'm usually at the gym, on a trail, or on a snowboard. I like problems with a clear finish line, whether that's a workout, a hike, or a stubborn bug.
